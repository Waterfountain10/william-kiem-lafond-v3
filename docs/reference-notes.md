# Supplied reference observations

These notes describe only what is visible in the supplied screenshots. They do not define the final composition; the forthcoming Figma mockup does.

## Rachel Chen screenshots

- **Type hierarchy:** a large, light-weight serif introduction contrasts with small neutral sans-serif navigation, metadata, and captions.
- **Background:** warm off-white paper with fine, low-contrast, evenly distributed grain; text is dark gray rather than black.
- **Spacing:** very generous top and side whitespace. A narrow left rail sits apart from a wide main canvas. Work imagery uses a spacious two-column rhythm.
- **Navigation:** persistent, understated left-aligned list. The active item uses a tiny warm accent marker/text treatment rather than a large control.
- **Lines:** the notes view uses faint card boundaries; line work is thin and quiet.
- **Accent:** sparse warm red/orange in active navigation. Project imagery carries most of the color.

## Shu Ding screenshot

- **Type hierarchy:** compact sans-serif titles and muted dates, with a small italic serif navigation rail.
- **Spacing:** content is concentrated in a narrow area at the upper left, leaving substantial empty canvas.
- **Navigation and lines:** a thin vertical divider separates navigation from content; dotted leaders connect article titles and dates; the active section has a restrained outlined state.
- **Accent:** essentially monochrome in the supplied view.
